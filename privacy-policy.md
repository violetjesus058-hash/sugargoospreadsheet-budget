# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: November 26, 2026**

This Privacy Policy describes how **Sugargoo Spreadsheet Budget** ("we," "us," or "this site") at [sugargoospreadsheet-budget.com](https://sugargoospreadsheet-budget.com/) handles information. We provide independent budget, category, brand, QC-reference, and shopping-guide links that lead to external destinations.

## 1. What this site does

This site is a navigation and referral directory, not a seller. We do not directly sell physical products, process orders or payments, inspect or store goods, arrange logistics, issue refunds, or provide after-sales support. Those functions belong to the external destination. Sugargoo and other brand or product names and images belong to their respective rights holders and do not establish official authorization, authenticity, quality assurance, or endorsement.

## 2. Information we collect

Browsing does not require an account. If you send an email, use a contact or request form, or otherwise contact us, we may receive the name, email address, message, and other details you voluntarily provide.

When you visit, hosting, security, and measurement systems may collect IP address, approximate IP-based location, device type, operating system, browser and language, referring page, landing page, click path, time on page, timestamps, errors, and UTM or other campaign parameters. We may also receive information about which budget, category, brand, or outbound link was selected.

Cookies and similar technologies may include browser cookies, local storage, pixels, web beacons, and tags. They may support preferences, analytics, link attribution, security, and performance. This site does not use these technologies to create a shopping account or accept payment.

## 3. Purposes

We use information to operate the directory; organize and improve budget routes; match visitors with relevant external categories and guides; measure outbound clicks, conversions, and campaign sources; maintain referral reporting; detect fraud, automated traffic, scraping, and attacks; troubleshoot and secure the service; and comply with law. Any personalized measurement or recommendation supplied by a configured tag is subject to that tag's settings and applicable requirements.

## 4. Google Tag Manager and providers

This site directly loads **Google Tag Manager container GTM-5Z4KX34X**. Google Tag Manager is a tag-management service that can load measurement or attribution tags only when those tags are configured in the current container. We do not state that Google Analytics, advertising pixels, or other SDKs are active unless they are actually configured.

For more information, read [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [Google's Tag Manager information](https://support.google.com/tagmanager/answer/6102821). Hosting, security, domain, and performance providers may process limited technical data needed to operate the site and may use international infrastructure.

## 5. Sharing and external destinations

We may share limited technical, aggregated, or pseudonymous information with providers that host, secure, measure, or maintain the site. For referral attribution, a linked destination may receive the referring URL, campaign or UTM values, click time, and browser-request information. We do not sell contact details submitted to this site.

A click can take you to a third-party website or app. That destination independently controls its cookies, SDKs, account data, payment, order, warehouse, shipping, refund, and support processing. Review its privacy policy and terms before submitting information or completing a transaction. We are not responsible for its practices.

## 6. Storage, retention, and security

Information may be stored or processed by our providers on servers in different countries. We do not promise one fixed storage location for all providers. We retain information only for operational, attribution, security, legal, or dispute-resolution needs. When the purpose is complete, we seek to delete or anonymize it, subject to provider settings and legal retention requirements.

We use reasonable measures including HTTPS/TLS, data minimization, access controls, restricted administrative access, and security logs. No transmission or storage system is completely secure. We will investigate, contain, and notify affected parties as required if a material breach is identified.

## 7. Rights and choices

Depending on applicable law, you may request access to, correction of, deletion of, or a copy of information; object to or restrict processing; withdraw consent; or ask us to stop personalized recommendations or referral measurement. You can manage cookies through browser settings, delete local site data, use private browsing, and adjust available advertising controls. Blocking cookies may affect measurement but should not normally prevent reading the site.

For a privacy request, email **privacy@sugargoospreadsheet-budget.com** with the subject "Privacy Request." We may verify the request and will respond within the legally required period. You may also use a privacy/contact link shown on this website if available.

## 8. Children's privacy

This site is intended for adults. We do not knowingly solicit or intentionally collect personal information from children. If a parent or guardian believes that a child has provided information, contact **privacy@sugargoospreadsheet-budget.com**. After verification, we will delete it when required and appropriate.

## 9. Changes and contact

We may update this Policy when our site, providers, tracking configuration, or legal obligations change. The current version and update date will be posted here. Send questions about this Policy to **privacy@sugargoospreadsheet-budget.com**. Direct order, payment, shipping, refund, or support questions to the external destination that handled the relevant matter.
